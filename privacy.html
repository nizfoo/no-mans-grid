No Man's Grid: Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

The short version

No Man's Grid does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved only on your own device: your mission number, your sappers and their ranks, the Roll of Honour, medals, daily results, settings and any board in progress. None of it leaves your device. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes it.

Sharing results

The "Copy result" button copies a short summary of your daily field to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services receive your IP address and basic browser details when the page loads. Their own privacy policies cover that. The game itself sends nothing.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised at github.com/nizfoo/no-mans-grid/issues.
